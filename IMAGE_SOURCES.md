# Gallery image provenance

42 new standalone images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Together with the original nine assets, the website contains 51 unique gallery images. These are AI-created design concepts, not completed client projects or evidence of actual installations.

All new images are native 1672 × 941. The requested generation target was 2048 × 1152, but the tool returned the smaller dimensions. JPEGs use quality 88 with no rescaling. The included walkthrough videos are Full HD (1920 × 1080). Original source PNGs were preserved during production; the distributable uses compact JPEGs.

## Prompt direction

Premium photorealistic architectural product/interior photography; one distinct scene per image; physically plausible epoxy craftsmanship, natural lighting and realistic reflections. No people, text, logos, watermarks or collages. Tables use a three-quarter product view; flooring occupies a substantial part of the composition. Nature floors use flat decorative imagery beneath clear resin, not actual holes or ponds.
